The pattern we keep seeing

The work is good. The connection is missing.

After years of transformation — agile, digital, org redesign — the same pattern shows up in organization after organization.

01

Delivery got better

Agile did what it promised. Teams ship faster and work better — inside their own walls. The methodologies are not the problem.

02

Everyone has goals

Product, engineering, marketing, HR — each chasing targets. But in most organizations those goals aren’t connected to what the company is actually trying to achieve.

03

Impact never arrives

So transformations succeed locally and fail organizationally. Same changes, same results — the definition of insanity.

A connected strategy gives everyone in your organization the standing to say “No, not now” to work that doesn’t serve the goal. Less wasted work. Lower cost of delivery. More value from everything you ship — starting immediately.

The framework

VAST doesn’t replace what you do. It connects it.

VAST — Value Adaptive Strategy — is delivery-agnostic. Agile, SAFe, Scrum, OCM, waterfall, or your own practice: VAST aligns the whole organization first (vision, top OKRs, every function), then connects the work you already do to the value the client receives.

Strategy developed

Vision, mission, focus areas, and a strategy map the whole leadership team built — not somebody’s assistant.

Cascaded to every team

Departmental maps every year, connected upward — so all work traces to the outcome, whatever methodology runs the work.

Measured & adapted

Impact measured continuously, plans adapted quarterly. Everything counts, or it doesn’t happen.
